Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2024

At Nexy Fans, we prioritize the safety and privacy of our users. This policy outlines how we collect, store, and protect your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

When you register on Nexy Fans, we collect the following data to provide a personalized experience:

Display Name Email Address Phone Number Gender Date of Birth

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Authentication: To verify your identity via Firebase Auth.
  • The 7-Day Rule: We track your lastUploadDate to manage account activity status.
  • Community Chat: Your messages are stored in Firestore for real-time delivery to group members.
  • Video Hosting: Uploaded videos are stored securely in Firebase Storage.

3. Content Safety

We do not share your private videos with third parties without your consent. However, all public uploads are subject to moderation to ensure they comply with our safety guidelines. We do not host or support illegal or non-consensual content.

4. Third-Party Services

Nexy Fans uses Google Firebase for database, hosting, and authentication. Their privacy policy applies to how data is handled at the infrastructure level.

5. Data Deletion

You have the right to request the deletion of your account and all associated data (videos, profile, messages). Please contact us through the Help Center to initiate this process.

6. Security

We use industry-standard encryption and Firebase Security Rules to ensure that only you can access or modify your personal information.
