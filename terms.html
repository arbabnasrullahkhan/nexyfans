Welcome to Nexy Fans. By using our platform, you agree to comply with the following rules and regulations.

1. User Activity (The 7-Day Rule)

To maintain an active and healthy community, Nexy Fans requires every user to upload at least one (1) video every 7 days.

Failure to meet this requirement will result in an automatic "Suspended" status for your account. You can request restoration via the Help Center.

2. Content Moderation

Nexy Fans is a safe social platform. We have a zero-tolerance policy for:

  • Explicit sexual content.
  • Illegal or harmful activities.
  • Non-consensual sharing of media.
  • Harassment or hate speech in community chats.

All uploads are subject to manual or AI review before becoming public.

3. Group Chat Limits

To prevent spam, users are limited to a maximum of 4 messages per day in community groups. This limit resets every 24 hours.

4. Termination

We reserve the right to ban any user who violates these terms or attempts to bypass our security systems.
